# AItonomy Foundation

**Open infrastructure for AI for Science and Science for AI.**

AItonomy Foundation is a nonprofit research community initiated by researchers from Stanford, Princeton, Oxford, and beyond. We connect AI researchers, scientists, and open-source contributors to build shared infrastructure for scientific discovery.

We focus on:

* Open scientific benchmarks
* Verifiers and evaluation tools
* Scientific environments for AI agents
* Collections of unsolved scientific problems
* Collaborative AI for Science research

Our belief is simple: **AI advances science, and science advances AI.** We build the infrastructure needed to accelerate both—and release it openly for the global research community.

Researchers, engineers, students, and domain experts are welcome to contribute.
