# AItonomy Foundation

**Open infrastructure for AI for Science and Science for AI.**

A nonprofit research community initiated by researchers from Stanford, Princeton, Oxford, and beyond. We build open benchmarks, verifiers, scientific environments, and collections of unsolved problems to advance AI and scientific discovery together.

Researchers, engineers, students, and domain experts are welcome to contribute.
